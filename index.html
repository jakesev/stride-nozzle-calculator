How to read this & important checks

Flow, pressure and nozzle size

L/min or US GPM is water flow. PSI is pressure. #2.5 is nozzle size, not 2.5 GPM from each tip.

A size #2.5 nozzle passes 2.5 US GPM at 4,000 PSI. At a different pressure it passes a different flow.

Two #2.5 tips make a combined size of #5.0. A three-digit size code 025 means #2.5; 020 means #2.0, 035 means #3.5 and 040 means #4.0. A full spray-tip marking can also include the angle; enter only the size part.

Estimates, not a pressure rating

The calculation assumes the full entered flow reaches equal, unworn tips. Hose losses, wear, pump behaviour and the unloader can change actual pressure. Check with a suitable gauge and the equipment instructions.

Pressure above the machine maximum is an unachievable theoretical demand at the entered flow, not a prediction that the washer can produce it. Use a suitable larger orifice.

Low pressure may stop a surface-cleaner bar spinning. Follow its minimum-flow, nozzle and pressure specifications. Never exceed the lowest rating of any component.

This calculator does not establish a safe cleaning pressure for sealed concrete or any other surface.

The calculation

Combined size = US GPM × √(4,000 ÷ PSI)
Each tip = combined size ÷ number of tips
PSI = 4,000 × (US GPM ÷ combined size)²
